   142→   142→============================================================
   143→   143→
   144→   144→Billing must belong to the BUSINESS / TENANT rather than the individual user.
   145→   145→
   146→   146→Conceptual model:
   147→   147→
   148→   148→User
   149→   149→   ↓
   150→   150→Business Membership
   151→   151→   ↓
   152→   152→Business
   153→   153→   ↓
   154→   154→Subscription
   155→   155→   ↓
   156→   156→Plan
   157→   157→
   158→   158→Reason:
   159→   159→
   160→   160→A business may have multiple members.
   161→   161→
   162→   162→Example:
   163→   163→
   164→   164→Business:
   165→   165→"ABC Store"
   166→   166→
   167→   167→Members:
   168→   168→Owner
   169→   169→Manager
   170→   170→Staff
   171→   171→Accountant
   172→   172→
   173→   173→The subscription applies to the business, not individually to every employee.
   174→   174→
   175→   175→Do NOT attach the primary subscription record directly to User unless a later explicit architectural decision changes this model.
