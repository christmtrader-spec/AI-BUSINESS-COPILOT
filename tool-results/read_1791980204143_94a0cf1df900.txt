     1→# Financial Semantics — What the Numbers ACTUALLY Mean
     2→
     3→**Audit doc:** Prompt 17 (r18) · **Scope:** every financial term used in the UI, API, reports and AI copilot
     4→**Method:** every definition below is traced to the implementation. Code is authoritative; labels are checked against `src/i18n/messages/en.ts` / `fa.ts`.
     5→

     7→
     8→## 0. The one-paragraph honest summary
     9→
    10→AI Business Copilot implements **simplified cash-based tracking for small businesses**, not double-entry accounting. There is no chart of accounts, no journal, no ledger, no accruals, no tax engine, no refunds, and no multi-currency conversion. All money is stored as **integer minor units** (`Int`), all arithmetic is integer arithmetic, and every aggregate is recomputed server-side from recorded transactions (`src/lib/reports.ts:1-10`). It is honest arithmetic over honest records — it is **not** a P&L, a balance sheet, or an accounting system of record.

    14→## 1. Storage foundation (applies to every term below)
    15→
    16→| Rule | Implementation |
    17→|---|---|
    18→| Money is **integer minor units** (e.g. cents) in `Int` columns | `src/lib/money.ts:2-7` (header law); `prisma/schema.prisma:179-180, 229-234, 272-274, 298`; SQLite has no usable `Decimal` — A08, `docs/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md:184-191` |
    19→| Floating-point forbidden for authoritative money | `src/lib/money.ts:2-7`; integer guards throw on non-integers (`money.ts:62-74`) |
    20→| Decimal → minor conversion uses **string math via BigInt** | `toMinor`, `src/lib/money.ts:32-53` (BigInt products at 44-45) |
    21→| Rounding: **half-up on the first discarded sub-minor digit** | `src/lib/money.ts:42-48` — `fracPart` keeps `digits + 1` chars (line 43); `firstDiscarded >= 5 → +1` (lines 47-48) |
    22→| Conversion overflow impossible | `Number.isSafeInteger` guard, `src/lib/money.ts:50-51` |
    23→| Input validation before any math | regex `^-?\d*(\.\d*)?$`, rejects empty/`-`/`.`, `src/lib/money.ts:35-38` |
    24→| Precision is business config, clamped 0..4 | `src/lib/money.ts:16-20` (`clampPrecision`); `Business.decimalPrecision`, `prisma/schema.prisma:45` |
    25→| Display formatting is presentation-only | `fromMinor` / `formatMoney`, `src/lib/money.ts:56-59, 80-95` |
    26→
    27→**Rounding rule, stated exactly:** `toMinor("12.345", 2)` keeps `1234` minor units, then inspects the first discarded digit (`5`) and rounds half-up → `1235`. Half-up is applied **only at the minor-unit boundary during input conversion** (`money.ts:47-48`) and at one display ratio (`avgSaleMinor` uses `Math.round`, `reports.ts:217`). Stored totals are never re-rounded: they are sums of already-integer values.

    31→## 2. Term definitions
    32→
    33→### 2.1 Revenue = SUM of `Sale.totalMinor` issued in the period (invoiced revenue, regardless of collection)
    34→
    35→- Computed: `revenueMinor += s.totalMinor` for every non-deleted sale whose `issueDate` falls in the window — `src/lib/reports.ts:141-148` (bucketing per day at `reports.ts:131`).
    36→- Basis is `issueDate`, **not** payment date: `db.sale.findMany({ where: { …, issueDate: { gte } } })`, `reports.ts:62-69`.
    37→- Month version: same semantics per `localMonthKey` bucket — `reports.ts:303-316, 342-343, 470-477`.
    38→- UI label is explicit about the basis: **"Revenue (issued)"** (`en.ts:655`, fa: "درآمد (فاکتورشده)" `fa.ts:658`); copilot snapshot field `revenueIssued` (`src/lib/ai/snapshot.ts:141`).
    39→- **This is invoiced revenue, not cash received.** Cash received is a separate figure ("Cash collected", §2.8).
    40→
    41→### 2.2 Sales = the count of invoices issued in the period
    42→
    43→- `salesCount` increments once per in-window sale — `src/lib/reports.ts:142-148`; per month `reports.ts:343`.
    44→- A "Sale" record **is an invoice**: sequential per-business number rendered `INV-0001` (`src/lib/finance.ts:7-12`), UI section titled "Sales / Invoices for everything you sell" (`en.ts:444-446`). There is no separate quotation/order entity.
    45→
    46→### 2.3 Expense = `Expense.amountMinor` recorded in the period
    47→
    48→- `expensesMinor += e.amountMinor` for non-deleted expenses in window — `src/lib/reports.ts:149-156` (query basis `spentAt`, `reports.ts:74-81`).
    49→- Created as `status: "UNPAID"` with `paidMinor` 0 (`expenses/route.ts:125-135`); schema `prisma/schema.prisma:265-286`.
    50→- An expense is a **vendor-facing cost entry with its own payment schedule** (payable + payments), not a ledger debit.
    51→
    52→### 2.4 Cost = `Product.costPriceMinor` — an inventory cost snapshot, NOT a COGS ledger
    53→
    54→- `costPriceMinor` is a per-product field (`prisma/schema.prisma:180`, zod bounds `schemas.ts:28`).
    55→- The only place it is aggregated is **inventory valuation at cost**: `inventoryValueMinor += p.stockQty * p.costPriceMinor` (`reports.ts:190-199`); label "Inventory value (cost)" (`en.ts:680`).
    56→- **There is no COGS.** Selling a product decrements stock and records the line at sale price (`sales/route.ts:199-222`); the product's cost at sale time is **not** captured on the `SaleItem` (its money field is only `unitPriceMinor`/`lineTotalMinor`, `prisma/schema.prisma:250-263`). Cost changes are not history-tracked, so "profit (issued basis)" (§2.5) does **not** subtract cost of goods sold.
    57→
    58→### 2.5 "Gross profit" / "Net profit" — what reports ACTUALLY show
    59→
    60→The app computes **two clearly-labeled figures** (`reports.ts:222-223`):
    61→
    62→| Field | Formula | Honest meaning |
    63→|---|---|---|
    64→| `netCashMinor` | `collectedMinor − paidOutMinor` (`reports.ts:222`) | **NET CASH FLOW**: actual money in (payments IN) minus actual money out (payments OUT) in the period. The primary honest number. Label: "Net cash flow" (`en.ts:661`, fa `fa.ts:664`). |
    65→| `netAccrualMinor` | `revenueMinor − expensesMinor` (`reports.ts:223`) | **"Profit (issued basis)"** (`en.ts:662`, fa `fa.ts:665`): invoiced revenue minus *recorded expenses*. It is NOT a formal P&L: no COGS (§2.4), no depreciation, no accruals, no tax provision, no matching principle. |
    66→
    67→**Plain statement:** the MVP shows **net cash flow** as the trustworthy bottom line. `netAccrualMinor` is a rough issued-basis indicator and must never be presented as accounting profit. There is **no formal gross profit anywhere** — no gross-margin figure is computed in `reports.ts` (nothing between revenue and expenses is netted; top products are ranked by revenue only, `reports.ts:166-176`).
    68→
    69→### 2.6 Payment = one cash movement IN or OUT against exactly one Sale or one Expense
    70→
    71→- Model: `direction` `"IN"` (customer pays us) | `"OUT"` (we pay supplier), `amountMinor`, `method`, one of `saleId`/`expenseId` (`prisma/schema.prisma:288-309`).
    72→- Exactly-one-target is enforced: `.refine(v => Boolean(v.saleId) !== Boolean(v.expenseId))` (`src/lib/schemas.ts:103-106`).
    73→- Direction is **derived server-side from the target** — `direction = "IN"` in the sale branch, `"OUT"` in the expense branch (`payments/route.ts:112, 135`); never client-supplied.
    74→- **No refunds exist**: `amountMinor: z.number().int().min(1)` (`schemas.ts:98`) — a negative payment is unrepresentable. Correcting money = deleting the payment (reversal, §3).
    75→- Methods are a closed enum `CASH | BANK_TRANSFER | CARD | OTHER` (`schemas.ts:66`).
    76→
    77→### 2.7 Receivable / Payable = outstanding sums over non-deleted records with `status != "PAID"`
    78→
    79→- Receivable: `SUM(totalMinor − paidMinor)` over `sale.findMany({ businessId, deletedAt: null, status: { not: "PAID" } })` — `src/app/api/v1/receivables/route.ts:16-26` (list capped `take: 200`, line 19).
    80→- Payable: identical over expenses — `src/app/api/v1/payables/route.ts:16-26`.
    81→- The same aggregates back the reports/copilot "outstanding" block: `sale.aggregate`/`expense.aggregate` with `status: { not: "PAID" }` (`reports.ts:103-112, 228-233`) — labeled all-time, outside the window (`snapshot.ts:138, 170-174`; `en.ts:684-685`).
    82→- UI: "Total receivable" / "Total payable" (`en.ts:608, 627`; fa `fa.ts:689-690`).
    83→
    84→### 2.8 Outstanding balance (per document) = `totalMinor − paidMinor`, floored at 0
    85→
    86→- Per-invoice: `balanceMinor: s.totalMinor - s.paidMinor` (`sales/route.ts:115`; detail `sales/[id]/route.ts:47`; month drill-down `reports.ts:508, 517`).
    87→- "Floored at 0" holds because `paidMinor ≤ totalMinor` is an enforced write invariant (§3, overpayment guard) — the subtraction can never go negative. The reversal path additionally clamps: `Math.max(sale.paidMinor - payment.amountMinor, 0)` (`payments/[id]/route.ts:39, 48`).
    88→- UI: "Balance due" (`en.ts:506`); payment form enforces client-side too: "Payment cannot exceed the outstanding balance." (`en.ts:601`).
    89→
    90→### 2.9 Cash flow = SUM(payments IN) − SUM(payments OUT) in the period
    91→
    92→- `collectedMinor` / `paidOutMinor` accumulate by `p.direction` over the window (`reports.ts:157-163`; buckets `reports.ts:133-135`; monthly `reports.ts:346-352`).
    93→- `netCashMinor = collectedMinor − paidOutMinor` (`reports.ts:222, 366, 499`).
    94→- UI: "Cash in vs cash out — actual payments received and made — the cash view of the business." (`en.ts:669-670`).
    95→
    96→### 2.10 Status (`UNPAID | PARTIAL | PAID`) — derived, never client input
    97→
    98→- Single source of truth: `statusOf(totalMinor, paidMinor)` — `paid ≤ 0 → UNPAID`, `paid ≥ total → PAID`, else `PARTIAL` (`src/lib/money.ts:106-115`).
    99→- Set only inside the payments pipeline (`payments/route.ts:123-129, 146-152`; reversal `payments/[id]/route.ts:39-43, 47-52`) or recomputed when an expense amount is edited (`expenses/[id]/route.ts:62-65`). Created as `UNPAID` (`sales/route.ts:180`, `expenses/route.ts:131`). Schema comment: "never client input" (`prisma/schema.prisma:214-218`).
   100→- Labels EN/FA match the semantics: Unpaid/Partial/Paid (`en.ts:440-443`, `fa.ts:443-445`).

   106→| # | Invariant | Where enforced |

   108→| I1 | `totalMinor === subtotalMinor − discountMinor` | Server recompute at creation: `sales/route.ts:148-161` (client totals never read; discount may not exceed subtotal, `sales/route.ts:158-160`) |

   122→## 4. Explicit non-features (what this app is NOT)
   123→
   124→| Non-feature | Evidence |
   125→|---|---|
   126→| **No double-entry accounting** — no ledger/journal/chart of accounts; balances are derived sums, not posted entries | Only `Sale/SaleItem/Expense/Payment` exist (`prisma/schema.prisma:220-309`); reports are recomputed aggregates (`reports.ts`) |
   127→| **No accruals** — revenue on issue date, cash tracked separately; no deferrals/receivables aging schedule | `reports.ts:62-69` (issueDate basis) vs `70-73` (paidAt basis); MVP scope lists no aging (`docs/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md:98-115`) |
   128→| **No taxes module** — no tax rates, no tax lines, no filings; a "tax label" is business settings copy only | No tax columns anywhere in `prisma/schema.prisma`; settings scope: "Currency, locale, timezone, tax label" (`docs/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md:103`) |
   129→| **No refunds / credit notes** — payments are strictly positive; corrections happen by deleting a payment (audited reversal) | `schemas.ts:98` (`min(1)`); reversal restores balance and deletes the row (`payments/[id]/route.ts:35-75`); UI copy "Payment removed — balances restored" (`en.ts:567-569`) |
   130→| **No multi-currency conversion** — one currency per business; no FX rates, no cross-currency totals | `Business.currencyCode/Symbol/decimalPrecision` (`schema.prisma:43-45`); A04 "MVP UX supports one primary currency per business" (`docs/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md:153-160`) |
   131→| **Not an accounting system of record** — product claims are "AI-powered business management and financial insight", never "accounting software" | Vision wording `docs/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md:16-28`; positioning guardrail of this audit |

   133→### 4.1 Party Statements (Prompt 39)
   134→
   135→Party Statements (customer/supplier, `src/lib/statements/`,
   136→`docs/product/party-statements.md`) are a **historical derived movement view**
   137→per party — they add NO new financial semantics to the product:
   138→
   139→| Term | Meaning (exact) |
   140→|---|---|
   141→| **Customer Statement** | chronological view of one customer's sales and received payments; balance = Σsales − Σpayments (receivable semantics) |
   142→| **Supplier Statement** | chronological view of one supplier's bills and made payments; balance = Σbills − Σpayments (payable semantics) |
   143→| **Opening balance** | the party balance immediately before the period start, from recorded history only (never invented) |
   144→| **Increase / Decrease** | movement columns; deliberately NOT "Debit/Credit" and NOT P&L — a sale row is not "profit", a bill row is not "loss" |
   145→| **Running balance** | server-computed exact integer `balanceAfterMinor` per row |
   146→| **Closing balance** | opening + increases − decreases; reconciles EXACTLY with the party receivable/payable when the range covers full recorded history |
   147→
   148→Statements do not modify revenue, expenses, net cash flow, receivables,
   149→payables, aging or inventory figures — they are an additional read-only lens
   150→over the same recorded rows. They are **not** a ledger, **not** an accounting
   151→system, and the FA wording uses صورت‌حساب (statement) — never دفتر کل
   152→(general ledger).
   153→
   154→### 4.2 Inventory Stocktake (Prompt 40)
   155→
   156→Stocktake sessions (`src/lib/inventory/stocktake/`,
   157→`docs/product/inventory-stocktake.md`) are an **operational inventory
   158→control** workflow. Their finalization touches physical inventory quantities
   159→ONLY through the existing adjustment semantics:
   160→
   161→| Aspect | Meaning (exact) |
   162→|---|---|
   163→| Quantity effect | `Product.stockQty` set to the counted value + one `InventoryAdjustment` row per non-zero-variance line (`delta = variance`, reason `Stocktake <reference>: physical count reconciliation`) |
   164→| Financial transaction | **NONE.** Finalization creates NO `Sale`, `Expense`, `Payment` or `Statement` row — revenue, expenses, net cash flow, receivables, payables and party statements are untouched |
   165→| Inventory value | May change ONLY because `stockQty` changes, under the existing `stockQty × costPriceMinor` convention (§2.4). The variance itself is **not booked** as expense, revenue, loss or profit — there is no shrinkage account to debit |
   166→| Ledger | **NO ledger.** No journal entry, no double-entry, no chart of accounts, no COGS, no valuation layer or cost history is created by a stocktake |
   167→
   168→The count line snapshots `systemQtyAtCount` server-side at count time and
   169→derives `variance = counted − system` (sign meaningful); finalization is
   170→atomic, rejects stale counts (409) instead of rebasing them, and zero
   171→variances produce no adjustment row at all.
   172→
   173→### 4.3 Purchase Orders (Prompt 41)
   174→
   175→Purchase orders (`src/lib/purchase-orders/`,
   176→`docs/product/purchase-orders.md`) are a **procurement commitment document**
   177→— the business's intent to buy. The document is explicitly NOT a financial
   178→term, and the definitions in §2 above intentionally have NO purchase-order
   179→case:
   180→
   181→| Purchase order is NOT | Enforcement |
   182→|---|---|
   183→| An **Expense** | No lifecycle transition (create/send/confirm/close/cancel) creates, updates or reverses any `Expense` row; expenses and payables aging never read purchase orders |
   184→| A **Payment** | No `Payment` row, no supplier paid/balance change, no statement entry |
   185→| An **InventoryReceipt** | No `InventoryAdjustment`, no `Product.stockQty` change on ANY transition — confirmed live by before/after snapshots over the full lifecycle (E2E group 22) |
   186→| A **PayableBalance** | Supplier statements / payables aging are built ONLY from Expenses + Payments (`src/lib/statements/engine.ts`, `src/lib/aging-report.ts`); purchase orders are structurally absent |
   187→| An **AccountingEntry** | No journal, no double entry, no COGS, no valuation — closing or cancelling a PO reverses nothing because nothing was ever posted |
   188→
   189→The document total (`Σ orderedQty × unitPriceMinor`, exact BigInt) is a
   190→procurement document figure ONLY: it appears on the document, its CSV and
   191→supplier/product history views, and never enters revenue, expense, cash,
   192→receivable or payable figures. UI labels are audited to never say
   193→expense/payable/owed (`docs/audit/financial-semantics.md` §5 wording check +
   194→static scan rule 72). Cancelling a PO performs no reversal of any kind
   195→(§63): there is nothing to reverse.

   215→## 6. Auditor notes
   216→
   217→1. `netAccrualMinor` is the only figure with upgrade risk: the name space ("profit") invites misreading as P&L net income even with the "(issued basis)" label. Any future marketing copy must keep the qualifier or drop the figure (see §2.5).
   218→2. Inventory valuation (§2.4) uses **current** `costPriceMinor` × current stock; historic cost is not snapshotted, so the valuation moves when a product's cost is edited. Documented behavior, not a bug — but it must not be called "asset value".
   219→3. `avgSaleMinor` is the only `Math.round` over a ratio (`reports.ts:217`); it can differ from any naive client-side recompute by ±0.5 minor units — the server value is authoritative.
   220→4. Receivables/payables list endpoints cap at 200 rows (`receivables/route.ts:19`, `payables/route.ts:19`) while the aggregate in `reports.ts:103-112` is unbounded — totals stay correct beyond 200 open documents, only the itemized list truncates.

   224→## 7. Goods Receipt (Prompt 42)
   225→
   226→A GoodsReceipt is a **physical inventory movement**, not a financial document. Full domain review: `docs/product/goods-receipts.md`.
   227→
   228→| Question | Answer | Evidence |
   229→|---|---|---|
   230→| Physical inventory movement | **YES** (posting only) | `tx.product.update({ data: { stockQty: newStock } })` + one `InventoryAdjustment` per posted line (`delta = receivedQty`, reason `Goods receipt <reference>`) — `src/lib/inventory/goods-receipt/service.ts` post path |
   231→| Expense | **NO** | no `expense.create` anywhere in the P42 domain (static scan rule 71); E2E pins Expense count unchanged (§216) |
   232→| Payment | **NO** | static scan rule 72; E2E §216 |
   233→| Supplier Statement | **NO** | statements derive from Expense+Payment only; receipts never enter the statement pipeline |
   234→| Payables Aging | **NO** | receipts add no payable balance; E2E §216 pins expense/paid sums unchanged |
   235→| Double Entry | **NO** | no journalEntry/ledgerEntry/debit/credit/chartOfAccounts/COGS vocabulary in P42 code (static scan rule 76, §225) |
   236→| InventoryAdjustment | **YES** — one positive row per posted line | `delta = receivedQty > 0` (§45); draft/cancelled receipts create none |
   237→| Product.stockQty | **YES** — increases on POST only | exact integer arithmetic, Int32 overflow guard (`GOODS_RECEIPT_STOCK_OVERFLOW`); DRAFT/CANCELLED mutate nothing |
   238→
   239→**Operational stock value only:** the derived figure `stockQty × costPriceMinor` rises when goods are received because the QUANTITY rose. That figure remains operational inventory value (§2.4 of this document) — it is NOT asset recognition, NOT a balance-sheet posting, and NOT an inventory valuation layer. No costing method (FIFO/LIFO/weighted average) exists.
   240→
   241→**Wording law:** the receipt UI uses receiving vocabulary only (receive / received / remaining). Persian labels use رسید انبار / ثبت دریافت کالا / مقدار دریافت‌شده — never پرداخت / هزینه / بدهی in receipt copy (§245; static scan rule 144). The PO close warning now states the receiving consequence explicitly ("Closing prevents further goods receipt. Unreceived quantities remain unreceived. No financial payable is created.").

   246→## 8. Goods Return (Prompt 43)
   247→
   248→A GoodsReturn is a **physical inventory outflow** — units physically sent back to a supplier from a POSTED receipt. Full domain review: `docs/product/goods-returns.md`.
   249→
   250→| Question | Answer | Evidence |
   251→|---|---|---|
   252→| Physical inventory movement | **YES** (decrease, posting only) | `tx.product.update({ data: { stockQty: newStock } })` with `newStock = stockQty − returnedQty` + one `InventoryAdjustment` per posted line (`delta = -returnedQty`, reason `Goods return <reference>`) — `src/lib/inventory/goods-return/service.ts` post path |
   253→| Expense | **NO** | no `expense.create` anywhere in the P43 domain (static scan rule 158); E2E pins Expense count unchanged (§186) |
   254→| Payment | **NO** | static scan rule 158; E2E §186 |
   255→| Sale | **NO** | static scan rule 158; E2E §186 |
   256→| Credit Note | **NO** | no credit-note model or wording exists; the domain contains no money fields at all (§7) |
   257→| Supplier Statement | **NO** | statements derive from Expense+Payment only; returns never enter the statement pipeline (static scan rule 159) |
   258→| Payables Aging | **NO** | returns add no payable balance and mutate no aging input; E2E §186 |
   259→| Double Entry | **NO** | no journal/ledger/COGS vocabulary in P43 code (static scan rule 162-family) |
   260→| InventoryAdjustment | **YES** — one NEGATIVE row per posted line | `delta = -returnedQty` (§44); draft/cancelled returns create none |
   261→| Product.stockQty | **YES** — decreases on POST only | exact integer arithmetic; negative persisted stock is a controlled 500 (never repaired §41/§164); `newStock < 0` impossible |
   262→
   263→**Correction language (§178):** Posted receipts remain immutable. A legitimate physical return uses a GoodsReturn document. Other inventory corrections must use the established authorized inventory-adjustment process. A positive GoodsReceipt cannot compensate for a stock overstatement, and a GoodsReturn cannot reverse an arbitrary historical stock mutation — it only documents a genuine physical return against one specific posted receipt.

   267→## 9. Sales Orders (Prompt 45)
   268→
   269→A SalesOrder is a **commercial commitment** — "the customer committed to
   270→buying these quantities at these prices". It sits OUTSIDE every financial
   271→authority until its two successor documents act. Full domain review:
   272→`docs/product/sales-orders.md`.
   273→
   274→| Question | SalesOrder create/confirm | Linked GoodsDispatch POST | Linked Sale invoice | Payment |
   275→|---|---|---|---|---|
   276→| Physical stock (Product.stockQty) | **NO change** | **YES — decreases exactly once** | **NO change** (§58/§122) | NO change |
   277→| InventoryAdjustment | **NO** | **YES — one NEGATIVE row per line** | **NO** (§58) | NO |
   278→| Sale / receivable | **NO** | **NO** | **YES — the ONE linked invoice (§52)** | NO |
   279→| Payment | **NO** | **NO** | **NO** | **YES — existing pipeline on the Sale** |
   280→| Customer Statement | **NO** | **NO** | via the EXISTING Sale statement law | via Payment |
   281→| Receivables / Aging | **NO** (§92/§94) | **NO** | via the EXISTING Sale law | via Payment |
   282→| Revenue | **NO** (the order is not revenue) | NO | YES — Sale.totalMinor as usual | — |
   283→
   284→**Two explicit sales paths (§7):**
   285→
   286→- **PATH A — legacy direct sale** (unchanged §120): `POST /api/v1/sales`
   287→  decrements stock + writes the negative adjustment exactly as before.
   288→- **PATH B — order-based sale** (new): SalesOrder → linked GoodsDispatch
   289→  POST (the ONLY decrement) → linked Sale invoice (receivable authority,
   290→  NO second decrement). The authoritative discriminator between the paths
   291→  is the `Sale.salesOrderId` relation — never notes or reference text.
   292→
   293→**No double-counting (§138):** a linked SalesOrder invoice must never
   294→perform the inventory decrement already performed by the linked
   295→GoodsDispatch documents (E2E §122/§124 verifies stock + adjustment
   296→invariants around issuance against non-zero baselines). Legacy direct
   297→sales remain separately supported; users must not record one physical
   298→outbound movement through both paths.
   299→
   300→**Linked-invoice deletion (§62/§121):** deleting a linked Sale is
   301→rejected (`409 SALE_LINKED_TO_SALES_ORDER`) before any restock mutation —
   302→the legacy delete-with-restock path applies ONLY to unlinked sales.
   303→
   304→**Wording law (§139):** the UI uses order vocabulary — EN "Sales Order /
   305→Confirm Order / Dispatched / Remaining to dispatch / Issue Invoice", FA
   306→سفارش فروش / تأیید سفارش / مقدار خارج‌شده / مقدار باقی‌مانده برای خروج /
   307→صدور فاکتور — never پرداخت/بدهی in order copy. COMPLETED means "fully
   308→dispatched AND invoiced", never "paid": the linked invoice follows the
   309→existing UNPAID → PARTIAL → PAID pipeline (§64).

   313→## 10. Sales Quotations (Prompt 47)
   314→
   315→A SalesQuotation is a **formal commercial offer** — "we propose these
   316→quantities at these prices, valid until this date". It sits one step
   317→BEFORE the order in the document chain and is NOT a financial term of
   318→any kind: the definitions in §2 above intentionally have NO quotation
   319→case. Full domain review: `docs/product/sales-quotations.md`.
   320→
   321→| Question | Answer | Evidence |
   322→|---|---|---|
   323→| Revenue | **NO — an offer is not revenue** | no lifecycle transition (create/edit/mark-sent/accept/reject/cancel/expire/convert) touches `Sale`; reports, copilot metrics and the dashboard never read quotations |
   324→| Invoice / receivable | **NO** | no `Sale` row, no receivable/aging input; receivables are built from `Sale` only |
   325→| Payment | **NO** | no `Payment` row, no paid/balance field exists on the quotation (subtotal − discount is the whole money model) |
   326→| Customer Statement | **NO** | statements derive from Sale+Payment only; quotations are structurally absent (`src/lib/statements/`) |
   327→| Inventory movement / stock | **NO** | no `InventoryAdjustment`, no `Product.stockQty` change on ANY transition — statically scanned (service + export contain no stock/finance vocabulary) and E2E-pinned byte-identical against non-zero baselines |
   328→| Stock reservation | **NO** | no reservation model; acceptance does not reserve availability |
   329→| Accounting entry | **NO** | no journal/ledger/COGS vocabulary anywhere in the P47 domain |
   330→| Conversion (ACCEPTED → CONVERTED) | **Creates NO financial record** — exactly ONE CONFIRMED `SalesOrder` (+ its lines) is the ONLY artifact, plus two audit rows | conversion transaction §13: NO Sale/Payment/receivable/adjustment/stock delta; order totals derived via the EXISTING SalesOrder domain functions |
   331→
   332→**The ORDER remains the fulfillment/financial authority.** Conversion
   333→copies the frozen offer terms into one legitimate SalesOrder; from that
   334→moment the quotation is a read-only source document — dispatch, stock
   335→decrement, invoicing, receivable and payment semantics belong to the
   336→Prompt 45 documents exactly as for any manually created order. An
   337→ACCEPTED-but-never-converted quotation has the financial weight of
   338→nothing: it is a recorded negotiation outcome, not a booking.
   339→
   340→**Validity display law:** `effectiveStatus: EXPIRED` on a past-validity
   341→SENT offer is a display verdict only — it never mutates a financial or
   342→inventory figure, because the quotation never fed one.
   343→
   344→**Wording law:** quotation copy uses offer vocabulary — EN
   345→"Quotation / Mark as sent / Record acceptance / Convert to sales order",
   346→FA استعلام قیمت / ثبت ارسال / ثبت پذیرش مشتری / تبدیل به سفارش فروش —
   347→never فاکتور (invoice) on the offer itself; the printable sheet is
   348→explicitly labelled as an offer, not an invoice.

   353→## Prompt 49 — detailed aging: single-count and wording laws (verified)
   354→
   355→- **Party identity:** aging groups (aggregate top parties AND the detailed
   356→  item view) key on the authoritative `customerId`/`supplierId`; identical
   357→  display names never merge groups (two same-name customers and two
   358→  same-name suppliers are live-tested).
   359→- **Single count:** an open item appears EXACTLY once in the detailed view —
   360→  Sale contributes `totalMinor − paidMinor`, Expense contributes
   361→  `amountMinor − paidMinor`; Payment contributes NO item (it moves the
   362→  paid side of its target); a posted SupplierBill contributes NO item (its
   363→  linked Expense IS the payable); SalesOrders/SalesQuotations/Goods*
   364→  contribute nothing.
   365→- **Wording:** receivables expose invoice AGE only (no invented customer due
   366→  date, no overdue claim); payables past-due = stated dueDate AND business-
   367→  local today strictly after AND positive balance ("due today" is not past
   368→  due; no due date is never past due).
   369→- **Reconciliation:** statement closing (full recorded history) == party
   370→  open-item total; detailed summary totals == Σ items == aggregate aging for
   371→  the same scope; partial payment reduces the open balance once; full
   372→  payment removes the item from every open view.
