## 1. Scope and method

One row per data category actually stored or processed by this product today. Every row was
written against the Prisma schema and the route audit — categories that do not exist (e.g. contact
leads) are listed as **NOT IMPLEMENTED** rather than omitted, so the inventory cannot imply a
collection that does not happen. What each row means:

- **Origin** — `U:` = values provided by a user; `S:` = values generated by the system.
- **Third-party processed** — only seams that actually exist in code. The single AI seam is
  `src/lib/ai/provider.ts` (z-ai-web-dev-sdk adapter, server-only). Payments use a provider
  abstraction with a mock provider and a Zarinpal sandbox adapter; production is NOT active and no
  merchant credentials exist. Email is a provider-agnostic boundary (`src/lib/email/index.ts`) with
  a dev console transport only — production email is **CURRENTLY NOT EXPLICITLY CONFIGURED**.
  There is no third-party analytics of any kind.
- **Exported?** — whether the category leaves the system through the implemented export paths
  (business JSON backup `GET /api/v1/businesses/[id]/export`; CSV reports under the `CSV_EXPORT`
  entitlement). Both are listed exactly as implemented; see `docs/legal/user-data-rights.md` §5.
- **Deleted? / Retention status** — what happens on account deletion and business deletion, and
  whether an independent retention policy exists. Full per-category detail:
  `docs/legal/data-retention-inventory.md`; operation-by-operation matrix:
  `docs/legal/data-deletion-matrix.md`.

## 2. Classification model

Four tiers. Assignment is an engineering judgment for handling decisions — it is not a legal
designation, and it does not map one-to-one onto any statute's definitions.

| Tier | Definition | Reasoning examples in this product |
|---|---|---|
| **PUBLIC** | Safe to expose without authentication; no harm from disclosure | Public pricing (`/pricing`, `GET /api/public/pricing` — plan names and prices with honest availability states); the public legal pages themselves; `robots.txt` |
| **INTERNAL** | Aggregated, derived, or operational data; harmful mainly in aggregate or to the operator's interests | Plan configuration and entitlements; AI usage metadata (token counts, no content); commercial funnel events; the AI cost ledgers and provider rate registry (operator-only, never tenant-visible); webhook replay ledger |
| **CONFIDENTIAL** | Business records or personal data; disclosure harms a tenant or a natural person | Business financial records (sales, expenses, payments); customer/supplier contact details; copilot transcripts; audit trail (incl. IP, user agent); member identities; legal acceptance records |
| **HIGHLY_SENSITIVE** | Credentials, secrets, or material that directly enables account or system compromise | Password hashes (bcrypt cost 12); session tokens (`NEXTAUTH_SECRET`-signed cookies); password-reset token hashes; AI provider credentials behind the server-only provider seam |

Note: `docs/security/data-classification.md` (Prompt 17) labels the CONFIDENTIAL tier
"SENSITIVE" with the same intent; this inventory uses the four names above and both documents
agree on membership. `next.config.ts` defines **no global CSP** — confidentiality of the tiers
above rests on the tenant pipeline, RBAC, and the other controls listed in that document, and the
CSP gap is tracked there.

## 5. Business data vs personal data

The tenant/business split is the backbone of this inventory, and it is deliberately conservative:

1. **Most rows in §4.2 are business data, not personal data.** Sales, expenses, products, and
   payment records exist to run a business and are owned by the business context, not the
   individual account. They are deleted through **business deletion**, and a member's account
   deletion does not remove them (`docs/legal/data-deletion-matrix.md` §4 — the owner-block makes
   this explicit: a user cannot delete business-owned data through account deletion).
2. **Business data can simultaneously be personal data.** `Customer`/`Supplier` contact fields
   (name, email, phone, address) may identify natural persons — sole traders, contact people at
   customer companies. Dual characterization is flagged, not resolved: final treatment is
   UNKNOWN-REVIEW REQUIRED pending legal review (the content registry itself carries the
   `legalReviewRequired` marker).
3. **The same person appears in several roles with different deletion fates:** as an account owner
   (User row — deleted with account), as a business member (membership row — deleted with account;
   the business survives), as an audit actor (link removed via SetNull, history row stays), and as
   the author of business records (business records stay; they belong to the business).
4. **Platform-level data is neither business nor tenant data**: commercial events, AI cost
   records, webhook ledger, and platform-level audit rows (businessId null, e.g.
   `auth.account_deleted`) persist across both deletion operations.
