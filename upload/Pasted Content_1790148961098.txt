- Is the business in a trial?

- What happens when a subscription expires?

3. BUSINESS-LEVEL BILLING DECISION
============================================================

Billing must belong to the BUSINESS / TENANT rather than the individual user.

Conceptual model:

User
   ↓
Business Membership
   ↓
Business
   ↓
Subscription
   ↓
Plan

Reason:

A business may have multiple members.

Example:

Business:
"ABC Store"

Members:
Owner
Manager
Staff
Accountant

The subscription applies to the business, not individually to every employee.

Do NOT attach the primary subscription record directly to User unless a later explicit architectural decision changes this model.

============================================================

19. Do not leak billing internals into unrelated business modules.
20. Keep billing logic behind a service boundary.
